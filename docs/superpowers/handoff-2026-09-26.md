## Next steps (the five options, the controller's recommended order)

### 1. Fire divergence gap (recommended first)
- **Problem:** in `fire`, Ember's RMS divergence at frame 60 is 4.0×, 26× and 28× Mantaflow's at 64³, 128³ and 256³ (`results.md`). This is 2b-4's one clear quality loss. In the smoke scenes Ember usually leaves less divergence than Mantaflow.
- **Suspect:** the Euler velocity backtrace used while fire burns. It is the only change from the smoke path, but the benchmark didn't isolate it. Flame vorticity and fuel clamping are other candidates.
- **First experiment, named in the diagnosis:** trace every grid with Euler, scalars included, as Mantaflow does. The diagnosis measured it stable to 256³ (peaks 10.6 / 9.6 / 12.0 / 12.1 m/s) but never measured its divergence. Also try `flame_vorticity = 0`, and `final` (8 substeps with RK2), to see what the gap tracks.
- **Scale:** a small investigation (a few hours), then possibly a `just bench fire` rerun (about an hour or more) and a spec decision for the user.

### 2. Fuel ratio above 1 at 128³ and 256³
- **Problem:** at frame 60, Ember holds 1.16× Mantaflow's fuel at 128³ and 1.42× at 256³. Ember's fuel is flat across resolutions, while Mantaflow's falls as resolution rises. At 32³ and 64³ the gap is explained: Mantaflow's advection gains fuel. At 128³ and 256³ it is not.
- **How:** the fuel budget method in `fire-diagnosis.md` §2 (emission, burn, advection gain and outflow, per frame). The resumable Mantaflow caches (fuel, react, fuel_inflow grids) are in `target/bench/fire-{128,256}/bake`. Check they still exist before relying on them.
- **Scale:** a small, analysis-only investigation. It may lead to a note in `results.md`, or show a real mapping problem.

### 3. Idle-machine reruns for risks (k) and (n)
- **Risk (k), preview with colliders:** 97.3 ms, measured under load. **Risk (n), fire preview:** 103.5 ms, over the 100 ms budget. Both are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-21-ember-solver-design.md` §6.
- **Needs the user to idle the machine first** (pause Backblaze and similar). Then run `just bench-presets` and `PRESET_SCENE=fire just bench-presets`, about 20 minutes. Record the load before and after.
- **Decides:** whether preview needs a cheaper fire path, for example skipping flame vorticity or using semi-Lagrangian scalars. If (n) still fails on an idle machine, that is a user decision.
- The older owed rerun `just bench-sweep 20,160` (ledger, "Open follow-ups after PR #5") can go in the same idle window.

### 5. Piece 3: export and handoff
